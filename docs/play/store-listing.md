# Play Store listing (copy and paste)

**App name (30 characters max):** MELK Studio

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Control your Bluetooth LED strip: scenes, music sync, sunset dimming and car mode.

**Full description:**
MELK Studio lets you control your Bluetooth LED strip with a simple, fast interface.

Scenes: one tap sets colour, brightness and effect for a situation, such as Movie, Reading, Sleep or Night drive. Auto picks a scene by time of day.

Colour and effects: a colour wheel, saved colours, and built-in ambient effects that change slowly and smoothly. Build your own effects from colour steps, with fade and hold times.

Music sync: lights follow the beat of whatever is playing on your phone. Music sync uses the sound your phone plays, so it also works with earphones. A microphone mode is available in the browser version.

Car mode: big buttons and a very dim screen for use while parked or on night drives.

Smooth and automatic: lights fade in and out. The app reconnects to your strip after it goes out of range, for example when a car is switched off and on. Brightness can dim automatically after sunset.

Shortcuts: add scenes to your home screen.

Your data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

This is an independent app. It is not made by or affiliated with MELK or any LED strip manufacturer.
