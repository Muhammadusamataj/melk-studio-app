# MELK Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MELK Studio is an app that controls Bluetooth LED light strips on your phone. This policy explains what data the app uses.

## Data we collect
We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting.

## Network use
When the app starts, it checks our server (a version file) to see if a newer version of the app's screens is available, and downloads it if so. This request contains no personal information. Like any website, our server may keep basic connection logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period to keep the service running.

## How the app uses data on your phone
- **Bluetooth:** used to find and talk to your LED strip. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.
- **Location:** used only to calculate sunset and sunrise times for the night-dimming feature. The app keeps your coordinates (rounded to two decimal places) on your phone only. You can turn this feature off at any time.
- **Microphone or device audio:** used only to measure loudness and beats so the lights match the music. Audio is processed in real time on your phone. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
- **Settings:** your colours, scenes, and preferences are saved on your phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Third parties
The app does not use any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
