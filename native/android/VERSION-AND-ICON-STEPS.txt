In android/app/build.gradle, inside defaultConfig:
    versionCode 2
    versionName "1.0.1"

Icons: copy everything from native/android/res/ into android/app/src/main/res/
(it overwrites the default mipmap icons). Copy AudioLevelPlugin.java into
android/app/src/main/java/studio/melk/app/.

Then in the android folder: Build > Clean Project, Build > Rebuild Project.
Uninstall the old MELK Studio from the phone before installing the new APK.
